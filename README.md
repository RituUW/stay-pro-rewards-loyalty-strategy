# Stay Pro Rewards — Loyalty Program Strategy

A product strategy case study for a points-based loyalty rewards program, built for a hospitality booking platform targeting small business owners who travel frequently for work.

## Overview

Stay Pro Rewards addresses a common retention challenge: acquiring new customers costs significantly more than retaining existing ones, yet many loyalty programs offer generic discounts that don't reflect what frequent business travelers actually need. This program ties rewards to practical, business-relevant perks — ride credits, groceries, event supplies — earned through bookings and referrals.

## What's Inside

- **Hypothesis & Validation** — core persona hypothesis (small business owner traveler) and a research-backed validation plan
- **MVP Scope** — defined in/out feature boundaries (points earn/redeem, rewards dashboard, referral program, notifications)
- **Use Cases** — three detailed user stories with acceptance criteria
- **Success Metrics** — engagement, redemption, acquisition, business impact, and operational KPIs
- **Roadmap** — phased Q1–Q4 rollout plan
- **Go-to-Market Strategy** — launch approach and positioning
